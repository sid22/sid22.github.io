About Me

Hello! I'm Siddharth, a senior software engineer currently working at Rubrik. I'm based in Bangalore, India.

As a programmer, I firmly believe in constant improvement as a general technologist and full-stack developer. I'm passionate about Open Source and have contributed to projects like OWASP. I enjoy building products that make a difference.

Outside of programming, I have deep interests in Astronomy and English Literature. There's something magical about both—the vastness of space and the depth of human expression through words. These interests keep me grounded and inspire creativity in my technical work.

I'd love to connect about anything related to technology, astronomy, or literature. Feel free to reach out via email or find me on GitHub and LinkedIn.
