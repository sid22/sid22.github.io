# Hello World

Welcome to my new blog! I've redesigned my personal website with a simpler, more minimal aesthetic. The goal was to create a clean space for sharing thoughts and ideas without the clutter.

## Why the change?

My previous site was built years ago and felt due for a refresh. I wanted something that:

- Loads fast and works everywhere
- Focuses on content over flashy design
- Makes it easy to write and publish posts

## The tech behind it

This site is intentionally simple:

- **Static HTML/CSS** - No build step, no framework complexity
- **Markdown for blog posts** - Just write `.md` files and they appear
- **GitHub Pages** - Free hosting with automatic deployments
- **marked.js** - Renders markdown on the client side

## What's next?

I plan to write about:

- Technology and software engineering
- Astronomy and the cosmos
- Books and literature
- Random thoughts and observations

Thanks for visiting. Hope you find something interesting here.
